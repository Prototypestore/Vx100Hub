Visual Design

This is my visual design system. It defines how my brand looks, feels, and communicates across every platform. The system is built around black, white, and a strong purple accent (#F049FF). The goal is simple: create a bold, clean identity that is instantly recognisable and easy to understand. Every colour has a role. Nothing is random. The design is structured to guide attention, highlight key elements, and keep the brand consistent across everything — from websites to logos to digital content. This is what defines the visual direction of my work.

Case Study Contact Us

The visual system is built around control, hierarchy, and purpose. Black and white form the foundation. They handle structure, readability, and spacing. This keeps the design clean and ensures everything is easy to scan and understand. The primary colour (#F049FF) is used for emphasis. It draws attention to important elements like the logo, headings, and interactive features. For example, it’s what makes key actions or brand moments stand out immediately on a page. The secondary colour (#C93ED6) supports the primary. It’s used for depth, transitions, and subtle variation in sections like gradients or hover states, without distracting from the main focus. Muted tones like #F5F5F5 are used to balance contrast. They soften backgrounds and prevent the design from feeling too harsh or visually overwhelming. This system is applied across all brand touchpoints — websites, UI layouts, and graphic design. It ensures consistency so the brand always feels the same no matter where it appears. In branding terms, this creates recognition. People don’t just see different designs — they recognise one system. One identity. The result is a visual language that is clean, structured, and intentional. Not decorative — functional.
