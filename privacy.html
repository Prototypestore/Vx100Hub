Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Vx100Hub ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website.

By using this Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide through our contact form.

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Selected service
  • Additional message details

We do not require accounts or collect payment information.

How Your Information Is Used

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Understand your request
  • Communicate about your project
  • Send confirmations or follow-ups

How Your Information Is Processed

When you submit the form:

  • Cloudflare Turnstile is used to verify that the submission is made by a real user and to help prevent spam.
  • Your form submission is securely processed through our website infrastructure hosted on Vercel.
  • Your inquiry is delivered to us via Resend, our email delivery provider.
  • We may contact you regarding your inquiry, requested service, or project discussion.

We do not intentionally store form submisstions into a database.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party service providers to operate our website and process contact form submissions:

  • Cloudflare Turnstile – spam and bot protection.
  • Vercel – website hosting and serverless infrastructure.
  • Resend – email delivery and contact form processing.

    • These providers only process information necessary to perform their respective services.

    Data Sharing

    We do not sell, rent or trade your data.

    Information submitted through our contact form may be processed by our service providers solely for the purpose of delivering and managing your inquiry.

    Data Retention

    Data is only retained for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry

    Security

    We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

    Your Rights

    • Request deletion of your data
    • Request no further contact

    Third-Party Links

    External links (like WhatsApp) are not controlled by us, and their privacy practices may differ.
